Leggacy privacy

Left Field Studio · Recipes worth keeping.
Effective 13 October 2026.

Your collection

Recipes, personal notes, photos, ratings, preferences and shopping lists are stored on your device. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. There is no Leggacy account or automatic cloud sync.

Choices that send information

You choose when to export, copy or share recipes and backups. A backup includes recipes, photos, notes and saved versions, but does not include shopping lists. Anyone you give the file to can read its contents.

Optional recipe generation requires connectivity and a separately configured creator or HTTPS server. Requests and selected recipe content may be sent to that service only when you submit them. Its provider may receive your IP address and request content and apply its own account, usage and retention rules. Do not include confidential or personal information. The external creator may require its own sign-in. Local cooking and recipe editing do not require sign-in.

Deletion and retention

Delete individual recipes within the app. Android Clear storage or uninstall removes the app’s local collection and preferences; export a backup first if you want to keep them. Delete exported files separately. Browser storage is separate from Android storage. External provider records must be managed with that provider.

Support

Project support. Do not post personal backups, photos, credentials or private notes in public issues. Privacy and support: leftfieldstudioapps@gmail.com.

Recipe information

Generated illustrations are representative, not photos of tested dishes. Generated and rebuilt recipes and adaptations are labeled; they are not kitchen-tested or certified dietary advice. Check ingredient labels and use safe cooking practices.
