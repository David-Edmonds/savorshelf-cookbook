Sources & public archive

This edition intentionally includes the author's recovered ChatGPT recipe archive and cooking notes. They are public content, not a private account or a complete ChatGPT data export. Your own later edits and imported recipes remain in your browser unless you export or share them.

Historical notes may be incomplete or superseded by later corrections. Home adaptations have not been kitchen-tested. Recipe images are labeled AI illustrations; missing matched photos are not replaced with pictures of unrelated dishes.

Wikibooks recipe text is credited within each recipe with source and author-history links and retains CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. Formatting, descriptions and substitution notes may have been adapted. Other recipe cards retain their own source and provenance notes.

The country directory is based on ISO country/territory and UN regional data. Inclusion in the directory does not mean a recipe collection is complete.

The site uses no analytics or advertisements. Opening a source or AI service is an external action. GitHub, if selected as host, handles ordinary hosting requests under its own policies. The app does not automatically send your local recipe library to an AI provider.
